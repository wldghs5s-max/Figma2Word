# Phase 29: HWPX Consistency & KS X 6101 Compliance

## 1. HWPX Element Pipeline Verification

In accordance with National Standard KS X 6101 (OWPML) and Hancom Office compatibility rules:

1. **Root Hierarchy**: Every HWPX document generated by `HwpxCanvasRenderer` includes:
   - `mimetype` (`application/hwp+zip`, uncompressed, stored at offset 0).
   - `META-INF/manifest.xml` and `META-INF/container.xml`.
   - `Contents/content.hpf` (package manifest).
   - `Contents/header.xml` (character shapes, paragraph shapes, fonts).
   - `Contents/section0.xml` (sections, paras, shapes).
2. **Shape Shadow KS X 6101 Alignment**:
   In Hancom documents, `<hp:shadow>` must appear after `<hc:fillBrush>` / `<hp:drawText>` and before coordinate points `<hc:pt0>`.
   Phase 29 renders:
   ```xml
   <hp:shadow type="DROP" color="#1A334D" offsetX="300" offsetY="600" alpha="191"/>
   ```
   Or `<hp:shadow type="NONE" color="#B2B2B2" offsetX="0" offsetY="0" alpha="0"/>` when no shadow is present.
3. **Corner Ratio Conversion**:
   KS X 6101 specifies corner roundness via the `ratio` attribute ($0 \le \text{ratio} \le 50$, representing percentage of half-width):
   $$\text{ratio} = \min\left(50, \max\left(0, \text{round}\left(\frac{\text{radius}}{\min(w, h) / 2} \times 50\right)\right)\right)$$
   For a pill button where $\text{radius} = \min(w, h) / 2$, $\text{ratio} = 50$, providing an authentic Hancom rounded capsule.

## 2. Regression Parity between DOCX and HWPX

Both engines now share identical geometric interpretation from the Canonical Canvas IR:
- Bounds scaling: uniform factor applied to both engines.
- Rotation: degrees aligned.
- Shadows: point coordinates converted to pt for DOCX and HWPU for HWPX.
- Fill opacities and stroke styles preserved across both formats.
